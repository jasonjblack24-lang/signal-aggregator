## Accounts Requiring Immediate Escalation

The first call needs to be diagnostic and low-pressure: acknowledge the gap, ask open questions about their current priorities, and find out whether the champion role has moved to someone new.

## Portfolio Summary

Of the 14 accounts, four require immediate attention before the relationship deteriorates further — Convoy, BeReal, Veev, and Fast all share the same pattern: red or drifting-red sentiment, product usage declining broadly, escalating or persistent support load, and CSM presence effectively absent. Veev stands out on timing (60 days to renewal); Convoy stands out on severity (nothing positive in the picture); BeReal has a competitive external threat that makes the absence particularly costly. Three more — Carta, Olive AI, and Plastiq — are in meaningful decline with negative sentiment but retain more relationship infrastructure to work with; the work here is diagnostic before commercial. Four accounts are genuinely healthy and growing but have been under-engaged during a window that matters: Turso and Clerk are approaching renewals now, Retool is the strongest uncaptured expansion signal in the portfolio, and Supabase is mid-funding-cycle. The remaining three (Descript, Linear, Vercel) are fine — the gap crossed the threshold but context is benign, and a normal check-in closes it.

The most counterintuitive finding across the portfolio: the accounts with the worst engagement gaps are not the highest-ARR accounts. Convoy ($570K), Plastiq ($649K), and BeReal ($788K) are all in serious trouble while accounts with lower ARR numbers like Olive AI ($159K) and Veev ($253K) are also flagged. Conversely, Retool ($410K) and Turso ($592K) are among the healthiest accounts in the cohort and yet have among the longer gaps. The gap signal is meaningful but it's the combination with usage direction and relationship status that determines actual risk — the gap alone predicts very little.
