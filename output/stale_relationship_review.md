## Churn Intervention
*Red sentiment, declining utilization, disengaged relationships, or critical renewal timing. The gap is actively making things worse.*

---

### BeReal — gap: 83 days · ARR: $788K · renewal: 111 days
Signal picture: Everything is moving in the wrong direction simultaneously. Infrastructure utilization is down 56% (trend 90d: declining, 30d: volatile), Logs down 48% (declining both windows), support is escalating with 8 tickets in the last 30 days and 4 open, sentiment is drifting red, and the champion has gone dark. The sole touch in the last 90 days was customer-initiated, which means they reached out — and the follow-up cadence is apparently zero.

**Does the gap matter?** Yes, significantly. A 83-day gap would be a yellow flag on a stable account; on this one it's a compounding factor in an already deteriorating situation. Each week without engagement widens the trust deficit and lets support frustration harden into a renewal posture.

**First conversation:** Don't start with the relationship — start with the support backlog. Four open tickets and an escalating severity trend give you a legitimate operational entry point that isn't "I noticed we haven't talked." Resolve something tangible first. Then, separately, have a frank conversation about what changed: the champion going quiet mid-contract with declining usage suggests something happened internally (org change, strategic reprioritization, product team disappointment) that hasn't surfaced in the formal record.

**Tension worth naming:** Instagram launched a competing disappearing-photo product right in BeReal's core market during this gap. The utilization decline and product_launch signal together raise a real question: is BeReal deprioritizing infrastructure spend because they're losing platform users? If so, the right renewal conversation is very different from a standard health conversation — it may need to acknowledge business headwinds directly.
